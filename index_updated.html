What is DIL SE?

DIL SE is a platform for authentic expression — not a talent show, not a competition, not a cultural programme with a script to follow.

Whether you sing, write, tell stories, make people laugh, rap, play an instrument, perform characters, beatbox, or have something completely unexpected to share — this is your stage. Nine ways in, one rule that matters most: bring your own voice.

  • Original work, performed live
  • Curators who help, not gatekeep
  • A stage that respects everyone on it
"Speak freely.
Create originally.
Respect everyone."

That's the whole idea. Everything else on this site — the categories, the rules, the registration form — exists to make that possible for every performer and every person in the audience.

Why DIL SE?

Four things it asks of every performer — nothing more.

Express

Share the thoughts, stories and creativity you don't usually get a mic for.

Create

Bring your own voice and your own ideas — not a copy of someone else's.

Perform

Take the stage and make it yours, for however long it's yours.

Respect

Be bold with your ideas. Be responsible with your words.

The DIL SE Code

Every category has its own rules — you'll find those on each act above. These nine apply to everyone, everywhere on this stage.

DIL SE OPEN MIC IS OPEN FOR ORIGINAL CONTENT ONLY. NO AI GENERATED, COPIED FROM ANOTHER ARTIST OR PLAGIARISED PERFORMANCES/SCRIPTS WILL BE ACCEPTED, NOR ALLOWED.

Be Original

Bring your own voice, words and ideas. That's the whole premise.

Be Respectful

No hate, harassment or targeted humiliation — of anyone, on or off stage.

Keep It Appropriate

No explicit sexual, discriminatory, hateful or obscene content.

No Provocation

Political or religious satire, campaigning or provocation isn't permitted here.

Respect Privacy

Don't reveal another person's personal information without their consent.

Respect Copyright

Don't perform someone else's work and present it as your own.

AI Isn't Your Ghostwriter

For poetry, storytelling and rap, the creative work must be substantially yours.

Submit On Time

Required content — lyrics, drafts, tracks — is due by the deadline for your category.

Perform What You Submit

What you submit is what you perform. No swapping in prohibited material on the night.

Languages Allowed

English, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi. No other languages.

Organizer Rights

The organizers may review, edit, reject or stop a performance that violates these guidelines. The organizers' decision on content eligibility is final.
